A prison routine is a machine, and in The Escapists 3 that machine will run in three dimensions for the first time. Team17's sequel is confirmed for PC and consoles in 2027, bringing five new prisons, more than 120 craftable or stealable items, and an XP system that follows your inmate from facility to facility. If you want the fastest escape rather than a slow, messy one, the work starts long before you pick up a shovel — and the fastest escape in The Escapists 3 will come down to how well you read a routine before you break it.

This guide breaks down what Team17 has actually confirmed, then maps it onto the planning framework that carried players through The Escapists and The Escapists 2. Nothing here is a magic button. ## What Team17 Has Confirmed About The Escapists 3

The game was revealed during the Triple-I Initiative Showcase, and the headline change is the perspective: The Escapists 3 is fully 3D, replacing the top-down view of its predecessors with fully realized correctional facilities. Team17's General Manager Harley Homewood described it as "a fresh perspective on a fan favorite," and the studio has been clear that this is a peek rather than a full reveal.

Here's what that means for anyone chasing a fast breakout.

| Confirmed feature | Why it matters for a fast escape |
|---|---|
| Fully 3D prisons | Camera angles, patrol coverage, and vertical space now matter. Blind spots that didn't exist in 2D may open new routes — and close old ones |
| Five distinct prisons | Each has its own routines, characters, and secrets, so a route that works in one facility won't transfer to the next |
| 120+ craftable or stealable items | More tools means more escape options, but also more chances to waste days hoarding the wrong materials |
| XP system that carries between prisons | Early facilities become training grounds — skills you bank there speed up every later breakout |

Worth noting: "fastest" in The Escapists isn't measured in real-world minutes. It's measured in in-game days survived, tools spent, and heat accumulated. A breakout that takes three days with zero guard suspicion beats a two-day plan that ends in lockdown.

## The Escapists 3 Fastest Escape Framework: Four Phases

Across the earlier games, the community-standard approach always followed the same shape: recon, supply, build, execute. There's no reason to expect The Escapists 3 to abandon that spine — the systems Team17 has described (routines, guard movements, jobs, inmate trading) are the same building blocks.

| Phase | Objective | Rough target | Cost of failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Recon | Map roll calls, patrol routes, camera coverage, and job assignments | Day 1 | Heat spike from being caught out of bounds |
| 2. Supply | Bank tools, materials, and trade goods through jobs and inmate deals | Days 1–3 | Confiscation during a shakedown |
| 3. Build | Craft the escape kit and stage it near the exit | Days 3–5 | Wasted materials and a missed roll call |
| 4. Execute | Break out during a distraction window with heat low | Day 5–7 | Lockdown, lost tools, full restart |

Those day ranges are estimates drawn from how The Escapists and The Escapists 2 pace themselves, not confirmed numbers. Treat them as a planning target, not a schedule.

### Why Recon Is the Phase Most Players Skip

Recon feels like doing nothing. It isn't. In a 3D prison, you're now tracking patrol routes that curve around corners, cameras with actual sight cones, and floors or ceilings you can approach from above. Spending the first in-game day walking the yard, timing the guards, and noting when the workshop empties will save you two days later.

If you want a shortcut, keep a mental log of three things: when each roll call happens, which job gives you access to restricted areas, and where the nearest wall to the outside actually is.

## Skills, Items, and Crafting Priorities for a Quick Breakout

Team17 says more than 120 weapons and items can be crafted or stolen, and that searching cells, trading with inmates, and completing jobs all build the resources you need. The trap is treating every item as equally valuable. It isn't.

| Priority | Tool category | Why it ranks here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Digging tools | The tunnel is still the most reliable exit in the series — start the wall early |
| 2 | Cutting tools | Fences, bars, and vent covers block otherwise fast routes |
| 3 | Disguises | Walking past a patrol is faster than avoiding one |
| 4 | Distraction items | Buy the window you need to dig, cut, or move without being seen |
| 5 | Trade goods | Currency for everything above — never stop trading |
| 6 | Healing items | Getting knocked out resets hours of progress in seconds |

### Specialize Your XP Instead of Spreading It

The confirmed XP system carries between prisons and unlocks new skills and gameplay options, letting your inmate specialize. For speed, that's a strong hint: pick one escape discipline and commit. A specialist who can dig faster and quieter will beat a generalist who's mediocre at everything, especially once later prisons scale up the difficulty.

## Reading the Prison Routine: Where the Real Clock Lives

Every prison in The Escapists 3 is a system with daily routines, guard movements, jobs, and inmates. The routine is your clock, and the fastest escape plans are built around it rather than against it.

| Routine window | Best action | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Morning roll call | Confirm attendance, then use the walk to scout new areas | Low |
| Work shift | Bank job income and collect job-specific materials | Low |
| Free time / yard | Trade, recruit co-op partners, test routes | Medium |
| Evening roll call | Stage tools near the exit point | Low |
| Lights out | Dig, cut, and move contraband | High |

The principle is simple: do your loud work when the fewest eyes are open, and do your scouting when you're supposed to be somewhere anyway. Players who dig during free time get caught. Players who dig at night and scout during the day get out.

## Multiplayer Speed Tactics: Splitting Roles

The Escapists 3 supports two players in local split-screen and up to four online. That's not just a social feature — it's a speed multiplier, because a team can run four jobs at once.

| Role | Responsibility | Best paired with |
|---|---|---|
| Scout | Learns patrol gaps and 3D sightlines | Engineer |
| Quartermaster | Runs jobs, trades, and banks materials | Bait |
| Engineer | Crafts and stages the escape kit | Scout |
| Bait | Pulls guards off route at the critical moment | Quartermaster |

Community reports from the earlier games consistently show that the hardest part of co-op isn't the escape — it's the timing. Agree on a signal before the breakout night, and make sure everyone knows where the exit is. The phrase "you distract the guard while I do this" is funnier right up until someone is sitting in solitary.

## Mistakes That Cost You Hours

- **Crafting in the open.** Crafting draws attention. Do it in your cell or a secluded room.
- **Ignoring roll call.** Missing one is the fastest way to a heat spike and a shakedown.
- **Hoarding instead of trading.** Materials you can't use are just evidence.
- **Digging before you check the geometry.** In 3D, a wall might back onto a solid foundation rather than open ground.
- **Having only one exit plan.** When plan A fails, a backup route is the difference between a delay and a restart.
- **Escaping solo in a four-player lobby.** If you have teammates, use them.

## FAQ

**Is there a confirmed fastest escape route in The Escapists 3?**
No. Team17 has confirmed five prisons with their own routines, characters, and secrets, and has explicitly said there won't be a single solution waiting to be discovered. Any route list you see before launch is speculation. The fastest escape will be the one you build from recon, not the one you copy.

**What should I level first for a fast breakout?**
Specialize. The XP system carries between prisons, so investing in one escape discipline — digging, crafting, or stealth — compounds over time. A focused inmate escapes faster than a jack-of-all-trades.

**Can you speedrun The Escapists 3 in co-op?**
Co-op is the faster option in principle. Two players can split recon and supply duty in local split-screen, and up to four can divide jobs online. The catch is coordination: a disorganized team is slower than a solo player.

**When does The Escapists 3 release and on what platforms?**
Team17 has confirmed a 2027 release window for PC and consoles. No specific date has been announced, so check [Team17's official site](https://www.team17.com/) for updates as the launch approaches.

The fastest escape in The Escapists 3 won't belong to the player with the best reflexes. It'll belong to the one who spent day one watching, day two trading, and day three quietly putting a shovel where nobody would look. Learn the routine, then break it.
