## The Core Stat Pillars Behind Every Inmate

Every Escapists game has leaned on a small set of attributes that govern how your inmate performs in the yard. Based on player experience with earlier entries in the franchise, those pillars are Strength, Speed, and Intellect — and each one quietly decides which escape routes are realistic for you.

Strength governs your ability to win fights and shift heavy objects. Speed controls how quickly you move, how well you avoid trouble, and how fast you can dig or sprint during a lockdown. Intellect unlocks crafting recipes, job performance, and the ability to talk your way out of a search. Treat these as the three dials on your inmate.

| Stat Pillar | Governs | Escape Routes It Opens | Cost of Neglecting It |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strength | Combat, heavy lifting, intimidation | Guard take-downs, brute-force wall breaks | You lose every yard scrap |
| Speed | Movement, evasion, dig speed | Tunnels, roof routes, fence climbs | Guards catch you mid-escape |
| Intellect | Crafting, jobs, deception | Disguises, fake passes, tool crafting | You can't build what you need |

Community reports from the earlier games consistently point to the same lesson: a balanced inmate finishes more escapes than a min-maxed one. That advice is likely to hold in The Escapists 3, especially with five distinct prisons that each emphasize different systems.

## How XP, Skills, and Jobs Feed Your Stats

In The Escapists 3, XP is the currency of self-improvement. You earn it by doing the things prisoners actually do: working a job, completing favors for inmates, winning fights, and pulling off the risky stuff. The store page explicitly notes that you take jobs to earn cash, trade with inmates for supplies, and turn everyday junk into escape tools.

That means your stat growth is tied directly to your daily routine. If you spend every in-game hour in the library, you'll likely see Intellect-focused gains. Spend them in the gym or the yard, and Strength and Speed follow. The routine is not background flavor — it is your stat allocation screen.

| XP Source | Typical Focus | Notes for Build Planning |
|---|---|---|
| Assigned prison job | Intellect and cash flow | Reliable, low-risk, keeps guards calm |
| Yard fights | Strength | Raises heat and guard attention |
| Favors for inmates | Mixed | Builds trust and unlocks trades |
| Crafting and tinkering | Intellect | Directly converts junk into tools |
| Digging and climbing practice | Speed | Slow, but compounds over many days |

A practical rule: pick two stats to push early and let the third ride at a functional level. Rolling call, attending meals, and staying out of solitary all protect your XP gains — a stat you never get to use is a stat you wasted.

## Stat Mistakes That Get Inmates Caught

- **Ignoring Speed entirely.** Digging is a timing game. Low Speed means more nights interrupted mid-tunnel.
- **Over-leveling Strength for no reason.** Combat is a tool, not the goal. Every fight raises your heat.
- **Neglecting jobs.** They are the safest XP and cash in the game, and they keep your cover intact.
- **Resetting your plan every prison.** With carryover progression, a stable core build outperforms constant rebuilding.
- **Skipping roll call.** It sounds trivial, but attendance protects the routine that generates your stats in the first place.
