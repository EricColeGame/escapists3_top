A gap that long usually signals a dead series. Instead, Team17 is coming back with the most ambitious version of the formula yet: fully realized 3D correctional facilities, more than 120 new weapons to steal and craft, and five distinct prisons to break out of.

## What's New in The Escapists 3? 3D Prisons, Crafting, and 120+ Weapons

The core loop has not changed. You wake up in a cell, follow a daily routine, and quietly work toward a plan that probably will not survive first contact with reality. What has changed is how much detail sits inside that loop.

Searching cells, trading with other inmates, and completing prison jobs all feed into a supply chain that ends with contraband in your mattress. This time there are more than 120 new weapons to steal or craft, and the guard rotations are systems you can actually study and exploit. The pitch is not "find the one correct answer." It is "build a plan, watch it fall apart, and improvise something worse."

### Five Prisons, Five Sets of Rules

Each of the five prisons has its own routines, characters, and secrets, which means your strategy has to reset every time you move. A trick that works in one facility may get you thrown in solitary in another. Expect to spend your first in-game days doing nothing but watching, counting, and taking notes.

| System to Study | Why It Matters |
| --- | --- |
| Daily routine | Roll call, meals, and work hours define your free time windows |
| Guard patrols | Shift changes create the gaps your escape depends on |
| Inmate network | Trading partners unlock items you cannot craft alone |
| Job assignments | Certain jobs give access to restricted areas and tools |
| Facility layout | Walls, vents, tunnels, and yards all become escape routes |

### Your Inmate, Your Build

Progression is now personal. You earn XP as you play, unlock new skills, and tailor your inmate toward the kind of escape you enjoy. Want to be a smooth-talking trader who never throws a punch? Want to be the inmate who tunnels out with a spoon and pure stubbornness? Both paths appear to be on the table, and mixing them is clearly encouraged.

## Co-Op and Multiplayer: How You'll Escape With Friends

The Escapists has always been better with accomplices, and the third entry leans into that harder than ever. You can escape solo, or team up with a friend in two-player local split-screen on the same couch. If your crew is scattered across the country, online play supports up to four players working the same facility.

| Mode | Player Count | Best For |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solo | 1 | Careful planners who want total control |
| Local split-screen | 2 | Couch co-op and shared chaos |
| Online co-op | Up to 4 | Big crews splitting jobs and roles |

Co-op changes the math in a good way. One player distracts a guard while another digs, and a third handles the trading. The catch is that four people executing four bad plans at once is exactly as messy as it sounds — and that chaos is arguably the whole point.

## FAQ: The Escapists 3 Is It Coming Out and Other Common Questions

The official window is 2027. Delays are always possible in game development, and no exact date has been locked in yet. Until Team17 confirms a specific day, treat 2027 as the plan rather than a guarantee.

**Will The Escapists 3 support crossplay between platforms?**

That has not been announced. Online co-op supports up to four players, and local split-screen supports two, but the publisher has not confirmed whether players on different platforms can share a prison. Community reports suggest demand for it is high, so it is worth watching for a follow-up announcement.

**Do I need to play the first two games before The Escapists 3?**

No. Each game drops you into a new facility with a new inmate, and the systems are explained as you go. That said, the older titles are a great way to practice reading guard routines and planning crafting chains — skills that will pay off immediately when the sequel launches in 2027.
