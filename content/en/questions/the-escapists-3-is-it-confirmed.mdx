## How to Tell Real The Escapists 3 News From Rumors

The biggest problem with the question "The Escapists 3 — is it confirmed?" is that the internet answers it badly. Search results fill up with videos titled "CONFIRMED!" and blog posts recycling each other. Here is a reliability ranking you can apply to any claim you encounter.

| Signal | Reliability | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Official publisher press release or website post | Very high | Company-controlled, verifiable, dated |
| Official studio or publisher social accounts | Very high | Direct source with accountability |
| Store page appearing on Steam or a console storefront | Very high | Requires real assets and legal clearance |
| Age rating from a ratings board | High | Ratings often appear shortly before a reveal |
| Trademark or domain filings | Medium | Companies file defensively and often |
| Anonymous forum "leaks" with screenshots | Low | Trivial to fabricate |
| YouTube thumbnails with all-caps "CONFIRMED" | Very low | Optimized for clicks, not accuracy |
| AI-written news aggregator posts | Very low | Frequently invent dates and details |

A simple verification routine saves a lot of disappointment. Check whether the claim links back to a primary source. Check the publication date — a lot of "news" is a reposted rumor from years ago. Then check whether a store page exists. If all three checks fail, you are reading speculation.

If a third game is ever announced, both will reflect it.
