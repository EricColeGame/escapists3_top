## The Escapists 3 PS5 Features at a Glance

Team17 has outlined four headline features for the game, and each one changes how you approach a breakout. The most obvious is the freedom to escape your own way — digging, sneaking, climbing, fighting, or disguising yourself to improvise a route to freedom. There is no single scripted solution; every prison is a system waiting to be understood and exploited.

The second pillar is the roster of five distinct prisons. Each one has its own characters, daily routines, secrets, and security gaps, so a strategy that works in one facility may fail completely in the next. You learn the rhythm of a place, spot the weak point, and hatch a different plan every time.

Crafting rounds out the formula. With more than 120 items and weapons to loot, trade, and craft, almost anything can become part of an escape plan — whether it should is another question entirely.

## Multiplayer and Crossplay: How The Escapists 3 PS5 Handles Co-Op

The Escapists 3 PS5 supports one to four players, with online lobbies capped at four inmates. Local play is limited to two players through split screen, which makes it a solid couch co-op option for a friend who is handy with a shovel — or hilariously bad with one. Online play is optional rather than mandatory, so the entire campaign can be tackled alone if you prefer a quieter breakout.

Crossplay is confirmed, meaning players on different supported platforms can team up in the same online session. That matters for anyone with friends spread across consoles or PC, since coordinating a multi-stage prison break requires real communication. Divide the jobs, sync your timing, and execute the escape together.

| Mode | Players | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solo | 1 | Full campaign playable offline |
| Local split screen | 2 | Same-room co-op for two inmates |
| Online co-op | Up to 4 | Crossplay supported |
| PS Plus | Required for online | Not needed for solo or local play |

A quick planning note: online escapes benefit enormously from assigned roles. One player handles the dig, another keeps a lookout, and a third gathers crafting materials. Randomly running around a cell block is how plans fall apart.

## Five Prisons, and What Waits Beyond the Wall

Getting past the wall is only the beginning. The Escapists 3 PS5 sends you beyond the fence into outdoor obstacle zones that test a different set of skills. You will navigate dense forests, climb across gusty canyons, explore ancient catacombs, and find your way through dangerous waters. These exterior segments turn a successful escape into a two-part challenge: break out, then survive the journey out.

That structure is a meaningful departure from a simple "reach the exit" objective. Escaping the cell block is a milestone, not a finish line, and players who sprint into the wilderness without supplies often learn that lesson the hard way.

### Earning XP and Unlocking Skills

Progression runs on experience points. As you earn XP, you unlock new skills and gameplay options, letting you tailor your inmate for the escape attempt ahead. Different strengths suit different prisons — a build focused on speed and climbing plays very differently from one built around strength and digging.

You can also customize your character and your prison cell. Cell customization is more than cosmetic: a well-organized cell makes it easier to stash contraband and stage the tools you need for the final push.

## Tips for Planning Your First Escape in The Escapists 3 PS5

Veterans of the series know that patience beats improvisation in the early hours. Use this phased approach when you load into a new prison.

| Phase | What to Do | Why It Works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Case the joint | Watch guards and learn daily routines | Predictable patrols create reliable windows |
| 2. Get a job | Take work and earn cash | Income funds tools, trades, and bribes |
| 3. Build a stash | Collect and hide useful objects | Keeps contraband out of shakedown reach |
| 4. Craft carefully | Combine items into escape tools | Everyday objects become questionable solutions |
| 5. Time the move | Trigger your escape during low coverage | Fewer eyes means fewer alarms |
| 6. Plan the outside | Prepare for forests, canyons, and water | The wall is only the first obstacle |

Two habits separate successful escapees from repeat offenders. First, keep a written mental checklist of what you still need — a shovel head, a disguise, a distraction. Second, never start digging until you know exactly where the tunnel ends. Player experience with the earlier Escapists games consistently shows that premature digging burns time and attention you cannot get back.

## The Escapists 3 PS5 vs Earlier Games

The biggest structural difference is the jump to 3D. The original game and its sequel used a top-down 2D presentation, where hiding spots and patrol patterns were readable at a glance. In 3D, you have to physically move your inmate to see what is around a corner, which makes reconnaissance far more important. Multiplayer also expands, with crossplay and four-player online escapes joining the established local split-screen option.
